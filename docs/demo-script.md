# StockWise 2–3 Minute 3MTT Demo Flow

1. **0:00–0:15 — Introduction**
   - State the problem: small shops need a simple way to know what they have, what is running low and what has sold.
   - Introduce StockWise: Smart Inventory. Simple Management.

2. **0:15–0:45 — Items / Products**
   - Open Products.
   - Search for Rice or Milo.
   - Show SKU, category, buying price, selling price and current quantity.
   - Briefly add/edit a product if time permits.

3. **0:45–1:15 — Stock**
   - Open Stock.
   - Perform Stock In on a product.
   - Perform Stock Out.
   - Show the updated quantity and Stock History.

4. **1:15–1:40 — Alerts**
   - Show a low-stock product.
   - Show the low-stock count on the dashboard.
   - Open Alerts and show out-of-stock/low-stock visibility.

5. **1:40–2:20 — Sales**
   - Open POS.
   - Search for two products and add them to the cart.
   - Select payment method.
   - Complete the sale.
   - Show the invoice.
   - Explain that stock is automatically reduced and the movement is audited.

6. **2:20–2:40 — Dashboard**
   - Show sales and inventory metrics.
   - Mention role-based access and responsive/mobile support.

7. **2:40–3:00 — Close**
